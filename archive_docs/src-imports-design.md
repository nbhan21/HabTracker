## Brand & Style

The design system is built on the principles of cognitive ease and rhythmic consistency. It targets high-achievers and professionals who require a tool that reduces mental clutter rather than adding to it. The brand personality is "The Quiet Assistant"—reliable, organized, and encouraging without being intrusive.

The visual style is **Modern Corporate Minimalism**. It utilizes high amounts of white space to separate tasks and data points, preventing the "information overwhelm" common in productivity tools. By combining a card-based architecture with soft elevation and a restricted color palette, the UI creates a calm environment conducive to deep work and habit formation.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for functional clarity. 

- **Professional Blue (Primary):** Used for primary actions, navigation states, and focus indicators. It conveys stability and competence.
- **Productivity Green (Secondary):** Reserved exclusively for "success" states, completed habits, and positive trend lines in progress bars.
- **Deep Indigo (Tertiary):** Used for data visualization and distinguishing between different categories of tasks.
- **Soft Grays (Neutral):** A range of cool-toned grays provides the scaffolding for the interface, used for borders, secondary text, and inactive states.

The background uses a subtle off-white to reduce eye strain during long planning sessions, while cards remain pure white to "pop" off the surface.
